About Us

The Art of Mehndi

Welcome to Rasmehina — where tradition meets artistry. We are passionate henna artists dedicated to creating breathtaking mehndi designs that tell your unique story.

Our journey began over a decade ago with a simple love for the ancient art of henna. Today, we've had the privilege of adorning hundreds of brides and guests with our signature designs, blending traditional patterns with modern aesthetics.

We use only 100% natural, organic henna paste — free from chemicals and PPD. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities.
